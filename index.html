Learn Ship Platform Diagnosis with Phoebe · Let's Ship It End to End

Two tracks from "last quarter looked fine" to a five-year diagnosis the board can act on

A B2B print-on-demand platform reviewed a rolling quarter for five years and never saw a thirteen-point margin break on two of its ten servers, because revenue kept growing. The chairman track (no code) drives the lookback window on real monthly numbers, walks what went wrong and what went right, and turns findings into decisions with an owner, a number and a review date. The analyst track builds every chart in pandas on a seeded dataset with planted truths: the data map and availability heatmap, the per-SKU margin bridge and change-point, products and baskets, merchant survival and store dormancy, frozen-history cohort retention, the promotion double edge, and the gaps the company must close. Everything runs from one shared reader, and every number on a page is produced by an executed notebook.
